Collecting The Correct Sample Is Easy: Don't Overcollect or Undercollect

An instruction pamphlet is included in your kit; however, we are highlighting the key steps here for emphasis.

  • After having a bowel movement, please wipe it as you normally would. Use the swab to gently collect a sample approximately the size of a grain of rice.

Use a small dab of stool which lightly but completely covers the swab.

  • The preferred method is to collect the sample from the stool on the toilet paper or by directly catching it on the swab. (Do not collect the sample from the toilet bowl,, as this may introduce contamination from the toilet environment and compromise results).
  • If needed, you may place a clean paper towel over the toilet bowl, pass the stool into it, and collect the sample from there.

If you have any questions or are unsure about any part of the collection process, please contact our team at help@vitract.com before mailing your sample. We are here to help you ensure your test is completed correctly and without delays.
